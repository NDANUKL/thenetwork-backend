# Scout Questionnaire Library

## A. Task information shown to the scout

Display above the questionnaire:

- Task reference and service type.
- Client’s specific questions and assigned checks.
- Site address, map pin and landmarks.
- Reference descriptions, documents, item lists or previous reports.
- Contact person and access arrangements.
- Restrictions and authorisation information.
- Summary of required photos, videos or supporting documents.

Display existing reference information rather than asking the scout to enter it again.

## B. Rules for answers and evidence

### Answers

- Every assigned check must allow an appropriate “Unable to check” outcome.
- Require an explanation for an incomplete check, but allow an existing location or access explanation to be reused.
- Show follow-up fields only when the selected answer requires them.
- Record a representative’s role or office when their information is used.
- Distinguish direct observations from information reported by others.
- Use item checklists for products, assets, milestones and document fields.

### Photos and videos

Each question must display an explicit requirement:

- **No photo or video required.**
- **Photo required**, with capture instructions.
- **Video required**, with recording instructions.
- **Photo or video required when a specified answer is selected.**

The scout does not choose which questions require media.

The coordinator must configure any additional requirements before assigning the task. Instructions must identify what to capture, rather than simply saying “collect evidence.”

### Reusing evidence

- Upload a photo or video once.
- Allow it to be linked to several answers.
- A linked image satisfies another photo requirement only if it clearly shows that question’s required subject.
- Do not require duplicate uploads or repeat recordings.

### Evidence that cannot be collected

For every required attachment, provide:

**“Unable to capture required evidence.”**

**Reason:**

- Photography or recording prohibited.
- Permission refused.
- Subject or item inaccessible.
- Site or item not found.
- Safety concern.
- Technical problem.
- Other — explain.

This records an evidence exception for coordinator review. It must not mark the evidence requirement as fulfilled.

Do not request photos of people, identity documents or unrelated personal information unless specifically authorised and necessary for the assigned check.

## C. Opening questions — shared by all services

### 1. Does the site match the assigned location?

**Select one:**

- Yes.
- No.
- Unable to confirm.

**If No or Unable to confirm:**

> Explain the location issue and where you carried out the visit.

**Photo:** None if Yes. If No or Unable to confirm, one photo of the location reached is required, subject to the evidence-exception process.

**Video:** None.

If the site cannot be located, allow submission of the attempted visit without requiring irrelevant service answers.

### 2. What access did you obtain?

**Select one:**

- Full access to the required areas or records.
- Partial access.
- No access.

**If Partial or No access:**

> Which areas, items or records could you not check, and why?

**Photo:** None.

**Video:** None.

Continue with checks that remain possible, including exterior observations. Mark blocked checks as unable to check and reuse this explanation.

# Service questionnaires

## 1. Land or plot check

### 1. Does the plot match the supplied description?

**Select one:**

- Matches.
- Partly matches.
- Does not match.
- Unable to confirm.

**Record:**

> Identify the visible features supporting your answer, including boundary markers, fencing or landmarks.

**Photo required:** Plot overview and the visible identifying or boundary features used in the answer. If no boundary features are visible, state this; do not imply that the legal boundary has been established.

**Video:** None.

### 2. What is on the plot, and how is it being used?

**Select all that apply:**

- Appears vacant or unused.
- Buildings or structures.
- Construction.
- Farming or cultivation.
- Business activity.
- Other occupation or use.
- Unable to establish.

**Record:** Brief description of the observed use.

**Photo required:** Images showing the selected use or the apparently vacant plot. Reuse question 1 images if they show this clearly.

**Video:** None.

Do not infer ownership or lawful occupation from visible use.

### 3. How is the plot accessed?

**Record:**

> Describe the access route and any restrictions or difficulties.

**Photo required:** One image showing the entrance or access point. If an obstruction is reported, include an image showing it.

**Video:** None.

## 2. Property or building check

### 1. Does the building match the supplied description?

**Options:** Matches / Partly matches / Does not match / Unable to confirm.

**If Partly matches or Does not match:** Describe the differences.

**Photo required:** Exterior overview showing the building and relevant identifying features.

**Video:** None.

### 2. What is the visible condition of the assigned areas?

Display only the areas selected in the task, such as exterior, compound, kitchen, bedrooms or bathrooms.

**For each area:**

- No obvious visible issues.
- Visible issues observed.
- Unable to inspect.

**If issues are observed:** Describe the issue and its location.

**Photos required:** At least one overview covering each inspected area. For reported issues, include a close-up unless the overview already shows the issue clearly.

**Video:** None by default. If a walkthrough is assigned, display one explicit recording instruction listing the areas to cover. Do not request separate videos for each area.

### 3. What signs of occupation or use did you observe?

**Options:**

- Signs observed.
- No signs observed during the visit.
- Unable to establish.

**Record:** Brief description of observations.

**Photo:** No additional photo required; link existing area images where they support the answer.

**Video:** None.

### 4. Are the requested fittings, features and utilities present?

Display the supplied checklist.

**For each item:** Present / Not found / Unable to check.

Show a function result only for items assigned for demonstration:

- Demonstrated without an obvious issue.
- Demonstrated with an issue.
- Not demonstrated.

**Record:** Differences, problems or reasons for no demonstration.

**Photo required:** Images covering the requested items found. Existing area photos can satisfy this requirement.

**Video:** Required only for functions marked “Video demonstration required” by the coordinator before assignment. The instruction must name the function to record.

## 3. Construction progress check

### 1. What is the status of each agreed milestone?

Display the supplied milestone list.

**For each:** Not started / In progress / Appears complete / Unable to assess.

**Record:** Brief description supporting the status.

**Photos required:** Images showing each assessable milestone or its assigned work area. One image may support several milestones.

**Video:** None by default. If a site walkthrough is assigned, require one video covering the specified work areas.

### 2. What has changed since the previous report?

**Show only when reference images or a previous report are supplied.**

**Options:** Visible progress / No visible change / Deterioration or removal / Unable to compare.

**Record:** Main changes only.

**Photo:** Use milestone photos. Require another image only when a reported change is not shown in those photos.

**Video:** None.

### 3. Did you observe unfinished work, damage or apparent defects requiring attention?

**Options:**

- Yes.
- None beyond the milestone findings already recorded.
- Unable to assess.

**If Yes:** Describe the issue and location.

**Photo required if Yes:** An image showing each reported issue. Link existing photos where sufficient.

**Video:** None.

### 4. What activity was taking place during the visit?

**Record:**

> Describe work underway and relevant materials or equipment. State if no activity was observed.

**Photo:** No additional photo required.

**Video:** None.

### 5. What did the site representative report about delays and next steps?

**Show only when representative confirmation is assigned.**

**Record:**

- Representative’s role.
- Reported delays and reasons.
- Reported next steps and dates.

**Alternative:** No representative available or willing to confirm.

**Photo:** None.

**Video:** None.

## 4. Business or shop check

### 1. What was the business’s status during the visit?

**Options:**

- Present, open and operating.
- Present and open, but no activity observed.
- Present but closed.
- Business not found.
- Unable to confirm.

If the business identity differs from the brief, record the difference.

**Photo required:** Storefront and signage. One image can cover both. If the business is not found, photograph the assigned location; reuse the opening-question photo if sufficient.

**Video:** None.

### 2. Were the requested goods or services available?

Display the requested-item checklist.

**For each:**

- Observed available.
- Reported available but not observed.
- Not available.
- Unable to check.

**For goods inspected, condition:**

- No obvious visible issues.
- Visible damage or deterioration.
- Unable to assess.

Record quantities only when requested. Explain differences or condition issues.

**Photos required:** Images covering the requested goods or service facilities physically inspected. One image may cover several items. Include a clear image of any reported damage.

No item photo is required for “Reported available but not observed,” “Not available” or “Unable to check.”

**Video:** None.

### 3. What was the visible condition of the premises and relevant equipment?

**Options:** No obvious visible issues / Visible issues / Unable to inspect.

**If issues:** Describe them.

**Photo required only if Visible issues:** Show each reported issue, using existing images where sufficient.

**Video:** None. If an equipment demonstration is required, the coordinator must add a specific demonstration check with its own recording instruction.

### 4. What did the business representative confirm?

**Show only when specific representative questions are assigned.**

Display those questions and record the answers and representative’s role once.

**Alternative:** No representative available or willing to answer.

**Photo:** None.

**Video:** None.

## 5. Vehicle check

### 1. Does the vehicle match the supplied description and identifiers?

Display the assigned fields, such as make, model, colour, registration and accessible VIN/chassis number.

**For each:** Matches / Differs / Unable to check.

**Record:** Differences.

**Photos required:** Vehicle overview and readable images of the identifiers specifically assigned for inspection.

**Video:** None.

### 2. What is the visible condition of the vehicle?

Display exterior, interior and dashboard.

**For each:** No obvious visible issues / Visible issues / Unable to inspect.

**Record:** Issues and assigned readings.

**Photos required:** Exterior views covering the front, rear and both sides; one interior overview; and the dashboard. Existing images may be reused. Add close-ups of issues not clearly shown.

Do not require images of inaccessible sections; record the limitation.

**Video:** None.

### 3. What happened when the requested functions were demonstrated?

**Show only when demonstrations are assigned.**

**For each function:**

- Demonstrated without an obvious issue.
- Demonstrated with an issue.
- Not demonstrated.

**Record:** Issue or reason not demonstrated.

**Photo:** None additional.

**Video required:** Record each demonstrated function. One continuous clip may cover several functions if each result is clear.

No video is required for a function marked Not demonstrated; an explanation is required.

## 7. Adult student enrolment check

**Prerequisite:** Required authorisation and institutional arrangements must be recorded before assignment. If disputed on arrival, pause the check and record the issue.

### 1. What enrolment details did the authorised office confirm?

Display only approved fields, such as student reference, programme, academic period and enrolment status.

**For each:** Record the confirmed detail or Unable to confirm.

Record the confirming office or role once.

**Photo:** None.

**Video:** None.

### 2. Does the confirmation match the supplied reference?

**Options:** Matches / Partly matches / Does not match / Unable to establish.

**If Partly matches or Does not match:** Identify the differences.

**Photo and video:** None.

**Supporting confirmation:** Before assignment, the coordinator selects the permitted method:

- Institutional written confirmation: attach the approved document or file.
- Authorised record photograph: capture only the specified fields.
- Verbal confirmation: record the office, role, date and response; no media required.

Show only the selected method to the scout.

## 14. General site visit

Display the coordinator’s approved custom questions.

### For each assigned question

**Outcome:** Checked / Unable to check / Not applicable, where permitted.

**If Checked:** Record the finding.

**Source:** Direct observation / Representative / Document or record / Combination.

## E. Submission summary

Before submission, show:

- Completed checks.
- Checks not completed and their recorded reasons.
- Required photos and videos attached.
- Evidence reused across questions.
- Required evidence unavailable and the recorded reasons.
- Missing answers or unexplained missing evidence.

Allow the scout to correct the form.

A visit with inaccessible checks or unavailable evidence may be submitted with explanations, but must be flagged for coordinator review. It must not appear fully verified.

If several services are assigned at the same site, show the opening and closing questions once and reuse relevant evidence across services. Add another location or access section only for a separate site or different access arrangements.
